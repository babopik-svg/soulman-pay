Privacy policy

Soulman Pay is a demonstration interface. It has no accounts, collects no personal data, and processes no real payments — balances, cards and tag codes shown here are concept figures only.

A booking enquiry is sent only when you choose to send it, through WhatsApp or email, directly to Tlamelo (DJ Soulman). Only the text you send is shared, under WhatsApp's or your email provider's own terms.

Hosting (Vercel) may record anonymised request logs, such as IP address and browser type, to serve and secure the site. Nothing else about your visit is collected or stored.

Questions: tlamelomo@yahoo.com

Cookie policy

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking of any kind. Nothing about your visit is stored on your device.

Fonts load from Google Fonts and the 3D library from cdnjs. Those content networks see your IP address when the files load, but they set no cookies for this site.

Because there is nothing to consent to, no cookie banner is shown.

Payment

Demo interface — no real payments are processed on this site.
